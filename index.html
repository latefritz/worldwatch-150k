WHERE I SEE ROOM TO IMPROVE

Getting families to stay is just as important.

At about 40,000 households, a 3.55% monthly cancellation rate would mean roughly 1,420 cancellations a month, assuming that rate applies to the full base. That's why I'd watch first-week viewing and retention, not just the signup number.

92.3% of subscribers are monthly; just 7.7% are annual. Courtney said choosing annual requires contacting member services. I'd explore whether making that choice easier can improve retention.

Reported marketing spend: $1.5 million. The time period and how much is WORLD Watch-specific still need confirming.

Important: 1,249 free trials are not the same as 1,249 paying subscribers. We still need trial-to-paid conversion and direct paid signups to know the full picture. That's a first-week question, not a reason to stall the plan.

02 / WHAT I'D DO NEXT

Two growth engines.
Running together.

I don't want six channels just so we can say we have six channels. I want the proven campaigns working hard while we build the next source of subscribers.

ENGINE 1 · PUSH WHAT WORKS

More of the right subscribers. Less wasted spend.

Meta already looks promising. I'd keep the winners going, but score each campaign by households that pay and stay—not the cheapest trial.

  • Compare the 7-, 30- and 90-day offers by actual paid conversion and retention.
  • Help families watch their first episode right away with simple onboarding.
  • Use existing WORLD newsletters, readers and shows to reach more good-fit families.
  • Keep podcasts and influencers where the full subscriber economics make sense.

ENGINE 2 · BUILD WHAT'S NEXT

Get discovered by families who haven't heard of WORLD Watch.

Parents ask Google and AI for news resources for their kids. They also look on YouTube. WORLD Watch already creates strong video content—we can put it to work in new places.

  • Build helpful pages that answer the questions parents and teachers really ask.
  • Earn recommendations from homeschool, parenting and education websites.
  • Turn selected episodes into searchable YouTube clips and Shorts.
  • Start small, track qualified signups, and grow what proves itself.
The AI + organic playbook ↗
03 / THE NEW OPPORTUNITY · AI + ORGANIC + YOUTUBE

Make WORLD Watch easier to find.

They already make a ten-minute news show every weekday. So I'd use content they have instead of asking the team to create a second news operation.

ON THE SITE

Answer real questions.

Start with the things a parent is actually looking for:

  • “Good news for middle schoolers”
  • “Current events for homeschool families”
  • “How do I talk to my kids about the news?”

Build a few genuinely useful, indexable pages. Use selected episode highlights, accurate summaries and family discussion prompts. Check titles, internal links, crawl access and video markup where it fits. Every page points toward a free episode or trial.

Measure: organic and AI referrals → free episodes → trials → paid.
OFF THE SITE

Earn real recommendations.

I'd reach out to homeschool organizations, Christian parenting sites, educators and curriculum publishers with something useful—not a generic backlink request.

  • Offer a strong free episode or a classroom discussion guide.
  • Find relevant educational resource lists and roundups.
  • Build a handful of genuine partnerships that introduce new families to the show.
Measure: partner mentions, referral traffic and paid households.
ON YOUTUBE

Use the videos already being made.

Test useful clips and Shorts from WORLD Watch stories that stand alone and make people want to see more.

  • Use clear, searchable video titles and descriptions.
  • Test explainers and playlists for civics, science and history.
  • Send interested viewers to the relevant free episode or trial.
Measure: engaged viewers → site visits → trials → paid.
WHY I THINK THIS IS WORTH DOING

I've seen a small AI channel turn into real revenue.

At my client PackagingSupplies.com, AI referral sales grew from roughly $1K–$2K a month to over $10K a month about a year later. WORLD Watch is a different business, so I'm not promising the same result. But I've seen what can happen when we pay attention to this channel and put in the work.

$1–2K → $10K+

The AI opportunity is a test, not a guarantee. Good SEO, useful content, credible mentions and accessible video matter; there's no magic schema that forces AI assistants to recommend a site.
